PHASE 1 SCOPE
1. SCENE ENGINE
   - Single fixed full-screen R3F canvas. Scenes are config-driven from src/scenes/registry.ts: id, title, label, image, depthMap, optional layers (far/mid/near), focalPoint (normalized x,y of the doorway or alley the camera dives through), parallaxStrength, fog color, ambient audio key.
   - Two render modes:
     a) LAYERED: for scenes with layers (hero, projects). Place far/mid/near on planes at different z-depths, with different parallax multipliers.
     b) DEPTH: for scenes with only a depth map (about, skills, experience, contact). One plane, with a shader that offsets UVs by (mouse offset * (depth - 0.5) * strength). White = near, so near moves more.
   - Idle state: mouse-driven parallax (on mobile use gentle auto-sway or device tilt), drifting fog planes, dust particles, a slow breathing zoom of about 1%. It must look like a still painting that breathes.
2. REGISTRY
   - Register all 6 scenes (hero, about, projects, skills, experience, contact). Build full interaction for only HERO and ABOUT now. The other four render as static parallax scenes with their titles.
   - Focal point defaults to tune in leva: hero doorway at about (0.63, 0.72), about-lobby doorway at about (0.5, 0.6).
3. ONE REAL TRANSITION: HERO to ABOUT
   - Scrubbed by scroll with GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis, so scrolling back reverses it.
   - Sequence: camera dollies toward the hero's focal point, FOV widens, fog density rises, a chromatic aberration pulse peaks near the middle, near layers slide past the camera and fade, the screen briefly goes dark, then the lobby fades in and settles to its static parallax state.
   - Same function must work for any scene pair so later phases reuse it: transitionTo(sceneId).
4. HERO CONTENT
   - Name "ROHIT PAUDEL" and role line "Game Dev / Marketing / Illustration" as a faded painted wall sign integrated into the scene, tagline underneath: "Passionate about coding, gaming, and everything in between."
   - Flickering "SCROLL TO ENTER" hint in the bottom center.
5. HUD
   - Compass-style progress indicator with 6 pips and the current section label in neon text.
   - Mute toggle (UI only for now, audio comes in Phase 5), a "Skip intro" link, and a menu button that lists all 6 sections and jumps with the same transition (for scenes not built yet, just crossfade).
6. LEVA DEV PANEL (hidden behind a ?debug flag in the URL)
   - Sliders for parallax strength, depth scale, fog density, transition duration, focal point x/y per scene.
7. QUALITY AND ACCESSIBILITY
   - Code-split per scene and preload the next scene's textures. DPR capped at 1.5. Target 60fps on a normal laptop.
   - Mobile: skip heavy postprocessing, swipe or scroll drives transitions, no mouse parallax.
   - Respect prefers-reduced-motion: crossfade instead of camera moves.
   - Keyboard accessible with visible focus states. Semantic HTML fallback layer with all text content readable without WebGL.
